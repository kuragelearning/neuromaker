Terms of use

This site offers practice on reasoning-question formats. It produces no IQ score, is not a psychometric test, and is in no way a substitute for assessment by a psychologist.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Purpose of this site

This site provides, free of charge, algorithmically generated exercises based on the question formats used in aptitude and intelligence tests. Its purpose is educational and recreational: to understand how such items are built, and to practise them. It runs entirely in the browser, with no account and no server.

2. This is not an assessment instrument

No IQ score, percentile, rank or level is produced, and none can be: an IQ score is a comparison against a representative standardisation sample, taken under standardised conditions, and this site has neither. The figures shown — accuracy, response time, progress — describe your performance on this site and nothing else.

In particular they cannot detect, confirm or rule out giftedness, a learning disorder, a cognitive impairment or any other condition. Only a qualified psychologist or neuropsychologist can carry out such an assessment, using a standardised battery administered face to face. This site must not be used as the basis for any decision about education, recruitment, schooling or health.

3. No warranty

The content is provided "as is", with no warranty as to accuracy, completeness, availability or fitness for any particular purpose. Items are generated and verified automatically; despite the checks in place, an error in a question, an explanation or an expected answer remains possible.

No result, no improvement and no success on any later test is guaranteed — practice improves performance on the exercise practised, which does not necessarily transfer elsewhere.
