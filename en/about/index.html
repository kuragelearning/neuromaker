Every item is generated from a seed, verified to have one answer, and explained afterwards.

Why there is no IQ score

An IQ score compares you with a large reference group tested under controlled conditions. This site has no such group, and a browser tab is not a testing room.

What it measures is you against yourself: accuracy, speed and level on each format, and how they move over time.

Practise what you enjoy. The figures here are a training record, not a verdict.

Privacy

Your answers stay in this browser. No account, no cookies, nothing sent anywhere.

Page views are counted anonymously, without cookies. Export, on the Progress page, is the only way your history leaves the browser.
